# Interview Introductions

## Option 1: Comprehensive & Professional (Recommended)

"Hello everyone, my name is Ajay. I'm originally from Haryana and currently pursuing my Master's in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at IIIT Lucknow. 

I come from a strong analytical background, having completed my Bachelor's degree in Honors Mathematics. This mathematical foundation has been incredibly valuable in my AI journey, giving me a deep structural understanding of the 'how' and 'why' behind complex neural networks and machine learning algorithms, rather than just treating them as black boxes.

Building on that foundation during my Master's, I've focused heavily on engineering advanced, production-ready Generative AI systems. Recently, I architected a complete **Multi-Tenant AI Chatbot Platform** with a robust FastAPI backend, custom RAG pipelines, and strict security controls. I also developed a highly specialized **Medical RAG Chatbot** engineered for domain-specific accuracy.

I'm truly passionate about bridging the gap between deep mathematical theory and scalable AI engineering, and I am very excited to bring my skills in building intelligent, real-world systems to your team."

---

### Delivery Tips:
*   **The Math Flex:** When you mention your Mathematics background, say it with confidence! Companies *love* developers who understand the core math behind the models.
*   **Pacing:** Take a distinct pause before transitioning from your education ("...behind neural networks.") to your projects ("Building on that foundation..."). 
*   **Eye Contact:** If it's a video call, look directly at the camera when you say "I am very excited to bring my skills..."
